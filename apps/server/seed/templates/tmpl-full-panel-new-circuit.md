Create a request from a tenant or the property manager to add one or two new dedicated circuits (new equipment, an appliance, or an IT rack) to a tenant panel that the site file shows is full or nearly full. Use the real panel, tenant, and any pending requests from the site file.

Vary:
- the equipment requested and its nameplate load;
- how urgent the customer says it is;
- whether someone has already "made room" in a non-compliant way (an unlisted tandem breaker or double-lugging);
- how much spare capacity the upstream transformer or feeder really has.

A good approach:
1. Get the equipment nameplate data.
2. Check the panel's spaces, recent load data and the feeder and transformer rating (R-LOAD-01).
3. Identify compliant options: a sub-panel, a new panel from spare feeder capacity, removing abandoned circuits, or listed tandem breakers only where the panel label allows them.
4. Give the customer a written proposal before doing any extra work (R-CUST-01).
5. Point out any existing violations you find.
6. After installation, update the typed schedule and labels (R-LABEL-01).

Red flags: double-lugging or unlisted tandem breakers to "make it fit"; skipping the load check; doing extra work without approval; leaving the schedule handwritten or not updated.
